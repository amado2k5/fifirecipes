About fifi.cooking

fifi.cooking is an open, searchable archive of the recipes of Dr. Fatma Alkawokgy (1943–2026), Egyptian culinary author and pianist, kept by her family. It is free to use and has no ads.

  • Source code: github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes
  • Related open standard for cooking safely: cookwala.ai
