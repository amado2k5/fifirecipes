fifi.cooking is a free public archive of Dr. Fatma Alkawokgy's recipes. There are no accounts, no passwords, no payments, no ads, no cookies and no analytics or tracking scripts, on the website or in the apps. This policy explains the little that does happen to data when you use the Service.

1. Who is responsible

The Service is run by Ahmed Abdel Aal on behalf of the family of Dr. Fatma Alkawokgy. He is the controller (in Canadian terms, the person accountable for personal information) and the person responsible for privacy, including under Quebec's Law 25. Contact: recipe@fifi.cooking.

3. Cookies and similar technology

The Service sets no cookies and uses no tracking pixels or fingerprinting. It uses your device's local storage only for your language choice. Third-party services you choose to load (for example the YouTube player) may use their own storage under their own policies. Because we do not track you, we treat Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals as already honoured.

4. International transfers

Our providers are based in the United States and process data worldwide. Where the GDPR or UK law applies, these providers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension, or on standard contractual clauses, as stated in their own policies. Data handled by us is stored in Canada and the United States.

5. How long data is kept

We keep no visitor logs. Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them, or longer if you ask us to keep a tribute, and are deleted on request. Providers keep their technical logs for the periods in their own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal information, including to know what we hold, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to withdraw consent, to object or restrict processing, to data portability, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. These rights come from laws including Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA and other U.S. state privacy laws, and Brazil's LGPD. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are about emails you sent us. To make a request, email recipe@fifi.cooking; we answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request comes from you. You may use an authorised agent where the law allows.

You can also complain to a privacy regulator: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information, your provincial commissioner, your EU data protection authority, the UK ICO, or the California Privacy Protection Agency.

7. Children

The Service is suitable for families and has a Cooking with Kids section, but it does not collect personal information from children or anyone else: there are no accounts, forms or chat, and the certificate name never leaves the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age set by local law, such as 16 in parts of the EU, or 14 in Quebec). If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, its code is public for anyone to review, and it holds no personal data to breach. Report security problems as described in our security policy.

9. Changes

We will update this page, with a new date, before any change in what we collect. Every change is public in the repository history.
