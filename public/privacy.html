Privacy

fifi.cooking is a free public archive of Dr. Fatma Alkawokgy's Egyptian recipes. There are no accounts, no payments, no passwords and no ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • We ask for no payment details and no passwords, on this site or in the apps.

Third parties the site can contact

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and sees your IP address and the pages you request, like any web host. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare may sit in front of some requests (for example the home-feed worker) and processes them as a network provider.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Optional location features: if you use features that find providers near you, the app may ask ipinfo.io for your approximate city from your IP address, or OpenStreetMap Nominatim to turn a place or coordinates into a city. Nothing is sent unless you use these features.

Feedback and tributes

Feedback and tribute messages open your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue. Security reports: see security.txt.
