Recipes are a family and cultural record

Most recipes were written for family members who already knew the dishes, and some come from older books and other cooks. Quantities, times, temperatures and steps may be approximate, incomplete or written for ingredients and ovens that differ from yours. They have not been tested in a professional test kitchen. Use your own judgement and cook food to safe internal temperatures.

Food safety

Follow current food-safety guidance where you live, for example from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and Health Canada, FoodSafety.gov in the United States, EFSA and your national authority in Europe, or the World Health Organization. Take particular care with raw or undercooked meat, poultry, eggs and fish, deep frying and hot oil, pressure cooking, home preserving and fermenting, and food for pregnant people, babies, older adults or anyone with a weakened immune system.

Allergens

Allergen warnings, and labels such as gluten-free, dairy-free or nut-free, are generated by a program that reads ingredient names. They can be wrong or missing, and they cannot see cross-contact, brand formulations, or ingredients hidden in stocks, spice blends and processed foods. “No allergen found” never means “safe for an allergy”. If you or someone you cook for has a food allergy or intolerance, read the label of every product you use and follow the advice of your doctor or allergist.

Halal, kosher, vegetarian, vegan and other dietary labels

These labels describe the ingredients listed in the recipe, as screened by a program and checked against published claims. They are not religious rulings and not certifications by any certifying body. Whether a dish is halal, kosher or vegetarian also depends on the products, brands, slaughter, preparation and utensils you use, which we cannot know. Consult the authority you follow.

Costs vary by place, season and shop.

Cooking with Kids

The Cooking with Kids recipes mark steps with a knife, heat, a blender or an oven for a grown-up, but every step should be supervised by a responsible adult who judges what each child can do safely. Check the allergens of every ingredient before cooking with children who are not your own.

AI-generated pictures and machine translations

Most pictures of dishes are generated by an AI image model as illustrations. They are not photographs of the recipe as cooked and may show ingredients, garnishes or presentation that the recipe does not include. Most translations, and some rewritten recipe text, were produced with AI tools and checked by software rather than by a professional translator. If a translation and the original disagree, the original (usually Arabic or English) is the reference. Please tell us about errors.
