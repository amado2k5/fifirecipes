# Security policy

fifi.cooking is a free, public archive of recipes with no payments, no passwords and no ads.

## Report a vulnerability or a malicious-content problem

- Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on
  [amado2k5/fifirecipes](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes/security/advisories/new).
- Say what is affected (page, URL, app), how to reproduce it, and the impact you see.
- We acknowledge within 3 working days.

## In scope

- fifi.cooking and its published files (`recipes.json`, `/data`, `/recipe-images`).
- The `worker/` Cloudflare Worker.
- Spam, defacement or abusive content in comments and tributes.

## Safe-listing and false positives

If a browser, filter or antivirus vendor flags fifi.cooking, report it through the vendor's
false-positive form and link to this file. The checklist is in
[docs/SITE-REPUTATION.md](docs/SITE-REPUTATION.md).
